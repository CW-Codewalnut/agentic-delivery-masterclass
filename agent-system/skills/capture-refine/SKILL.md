# Capture & Refine router

## Inputs
Read the role contract at [`../../roles/capture-refine.md`](../../roles/capture-refine.md) and the exact work artifacts it requires.

## Route
1. Open `capture-intake-and-gaps` to establish evidence and decisions.
2. Open `capture-engineer-interview` only for engineering-observable gaps.
3. Open `capture-prd-handoff` when the gap register is explicit enough to draft.

## Output
Finish at engineer completeness review; Product approval remains separate.
